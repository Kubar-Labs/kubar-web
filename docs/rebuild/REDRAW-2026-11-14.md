## 1. The rules behind the redraw

1. **Depth where something stacks or builds.** Documents stack into a record, foundations build into a venue, layers sit behind a product. Those are drawn in isometric projection.
2. **Flat where the reader compares or reads.** The to-scale bar, the who-takes-part map, glossaries, registers a credit committee looks things up in, forms and legal text stay flat.
3. **A shadow means an object.** Sheets, parties on the map, routes you can take, the one item picked out of a set. Never use a shadow or a card only to group text.
4. **No set of identical cards.** Where three or four things are compared, one is raised and the rest are quiet columns.
5. **No hairline between sections.** Sections are separated by space. The header keeps its bottom rule.
6. **Monospace only where it carries a reference:** record and receivable numbers, states, step and document numbers, figures, footnote-level captions on drawings. Other small labels use `Sans/Fine`.
7. **Numbers only on real sequences.** The venue's three points and the SDK's four rules lose their 01–04.

## 4. How to build the isometric drawings

All drawings use one projection. A flat rectangle becomes a sheet with a 2D matrix, so no 3D context is needed:

```css
.iso { transform-origin: 0 0; transform: matrix(0.866, 0.5, -0.866, 0.5, 0, 0); }
```

- **Sheets** (documents, receivables): a white rectangle with the matrix above, a 1 px border, an elevation shadow, and a copy of itself 4 px lower in `rule/default` (rani for the current sheet) to give thickness. Stack by translating each sheet up the screen: 46 px apart on Home, 38 px on How it works.
- **Blocks** (foundations, layers, steps): three SVG polygons per block, top face white, left face `petrol/100`, right face `petrol/50`. Kubar Protocol's layer is petrol/900 with `petrol/850` and `petrol/800` sides. A planned item is the same outline dashed `6 5` with no fill.
- **Ground shadow:** one blurred ellipse under each drawing, petrol at 20%.
- **Labels are real text**, not part of the picture. Each drawing is an ordered or unordered list in the DOM whose items are positioned beside the art on desktop and listed under it on phone. The art itself is `aria-hidden`. Screen readers and reduced-motion users get the list.
- Build each drawing as inline SVG or positioned HTML. No canvas, no WebGL, no image files.

## 7. Motion

Use the existing motion tokens (120 ms hover, 200 ms openings, 320 ms arrivals, 80 ms stagger, the standard ease-out). Rules for everything below:

- Each drawing has one authored movement. It plays once, when 40% of the drawing is in view, and stays in its end state.
- No fade-up applied to sections in general. No parallax. No looping, pulsing or spinning. No count-up numbers.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity` only.
- Every hover effect has a keyboard-focus and a tap equivalent.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: every drawing renders in its end state with no movement, and hover lifts become a colour change.
